AI Chat

A small AI that runs on your device. No API key or subscription.
First use downloads a model that can take several minutes and substantial storage. Requires WebGPU and enough device memory. Model files are fetched from external providers; your prompts are processed here on your device.

Click Start when you’re ready. Chrome or Edge with WebGPU is recommended.

Arithmetic expressions use a built-in calculator. Other answers come from a small AI model and may be incorrect. It cannot browse the web. Keep a copy of anything you want to save; chats clear when this page reloads.
